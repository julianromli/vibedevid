## Executive Summary

"Vibe coding" has exploded from near-zero searches in early 2025 to **~110,000 monthly US searches** and significant global volume, with APAC (including Indonesia) leading per-capita adoption. Collins Dictionary named it **Word of the Year 2025**. The term was coined by Andrej Karpathy in February 2025 and has since spawned a $4.7 billion industry projected to reach $12.3B by 2027.

For VibeDev ID, the opportunity is twofold:
1. **Indonesian-language keywords** face low competition and high growth — the Indonesian vibe coding ecosystem is nascent with only 3-4 direct competitors (vibecoders.id, vibecoding.id, VibeCode Hub/Sanbercode, DEVMODE).
2. **English long-tail keywords** around specific tools (Claude Code, Cursor, OpenCode, Kiro, Trae) have manageable difficulty and strong commercial intent.

This report identifies **120+ keywords** across **12 clusters**, mapped to specific site pages, with a prioritization matrix for quick wins, strategic targets, and long-tail plays.
